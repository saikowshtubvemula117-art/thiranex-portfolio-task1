Introduction

A developer who starts with the structure, not the styling

I am a third-year Computer Science student who enjoys turning ambiguous problems into software other people can actually use. My work sits at the intersection of the front end and the cloud: I care about semantic HTML, keyboard-first interaction, and shipping features that hold up under real data.

My strongest interest is accessible web engineering. I have rebuilt one of my own dashboards twice — first for performance, then for screen-reader usability — and the accessibility pass is what made the product usable for far more people.

Outside coursework I contribute small accessibility fixes to open-source projects, write short notes on what I learn, and mentor first-year students on HTML semantics.

Featured projects

The three projects I am proudest of. Each one started as a problem I personally hit, not as a tutorial to follow.

Smart Train Ticket Booking & Checking

01

A smart railway application for checking live train locations, booking train and platform tickets, and transferring a booked ticket to another available train when the original one is missed.

Key features

  • Live train tracking between selected stations, refreshed in real time.
  • Booking across train and platform tickets, with fare difference calculated automatically.
  • QR-based e-tickets that a checker can scan and verify at the platform.
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • PostgreSQL
  • Razorpay
Deployed

Integrated GIS-Based Land Governance

02

A unified GIS platform that connects land records, registration, planning, taxation, utilities and spatial data through a single common parcel identity.

Key features

  • Parcel-centric GIS that links every record to one land parcel.
  • AI land-change detection that analyses satellite imagery and raises alerts.
  • Citizen and government services with dashboards and grievance tracking.
  • PostGIS
  • Leaflet.js
  • Python
  • Django
In progress

Praveen Hospital Appointment System

03

A smart digital platform that simplifies hospital booking: patients find the right doctor, check real-time slots and book an appointment online.

Key features

  • Mobile number with OTP login for secure access.
  • Real-time appointment date and time-slot selection, with UPI or pay-at-hospital.
  • Appointment history and full booking information inside the app.
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • MySQL
  • Twilio
Deployed
