About me

Third-year CSE student, building software for the web and AI

I am a third-year B.Tech student in Computer Science Engineering, based in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, with a strong interest in software development, web technologies, and artificial intelligence.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical applications and learning by building projects. During my academic journey, I have worked with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, Flutter, and SQL, while exploring areas including AI/ML and full-stack development.

I have developed and explored projects that solve real-world problems, including applications for train ticket management, hospital appointment booking, travel planning, and fashion recommendations. These projects have helped me improve my problem-solving, UI design, programming, and teamwork skills.

I believe that the best way to learn technology is by building, testing, making mistakes, and improving. I am continuously working on my technical skills and looking for opportunities to learn from real-world projects, hackathons, and collaborative development.

Outside academics, I enjoy exploring new technologies, participating in hackathons, and experimenting with new project ideas. My goal is to grow as a software developer and build technology that is useful, reliable, and easy to use.
